# Skill: root-cause method (quantify -> localize -> decompose -> conclude)
1. Quantify: how big is the change, and exactly when did it start? (a step change
   points to an event; a drift points to a process.)
2. Localize: segment by each dimension (region, product, channel, source) and find
   where the delta concentrates. A change confined to one segment is a strong lead.
3. Decompose: is it VOLUME (count changed) or RATE (per-unit changed)? Mix-shift
   (composition changed) or true movement?
4. Rule out artifacts FIRST (see data_vs_reality), then business/external causes.
5. Conclude with the most-supported cause, its share of the delta, and confidence.
