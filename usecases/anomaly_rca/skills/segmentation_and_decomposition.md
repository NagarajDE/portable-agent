# Skill: segmentation & decomposition
- Slice by one dimension at a time; the segment carrying most of the delta is the
  lead. Contribution ≈ Δ(segment) summed to the total Δ.
- Volume vs rate: total = volume × rate. Decompose to see which moved — a 30% drop
  from fewer sessions is a different problem than the same drop from lower
  conversion.
- Mix-shift (Simpson's): the overall rate can move purely because the MIX of
  segments changed, even if every segment held steady. Compare distributions, not
  just totals.
